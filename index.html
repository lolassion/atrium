Frequently asked questions

A few things you might be wondering before you get started.

Is Atrium free?

Yes. No account, no subscription and nothing to sign into.

Does it replace Steam?

No. Atrium launches games through their own launchers, so achievements, cloud saves and friends all keep working.

Why does Windows warn me?

The app isn't code-signed yet. Choose More info → Run anyway once and it won't ask again.

Does it need the internet?

No. Artwork comes from your launchers' local caches, and the web is only a fallback.

Which launchers work?

Steam, Epic Games, GOG and Ubisoft Connect, plus any .exe you add.

Where are my settings?

In one small file at %APPDATA%\Atrium. Delete it to start fresh.

Portable or installer?

Either. The portable .exe runs from anywhere, and the installer adds Start Menu shortcuts.

Can I hide games?

Right-click a cover and choose Hide from library. The Hidden tab brings it back.
