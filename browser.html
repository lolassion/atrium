Locked down underneath

The quiet surface sits on a hardened Chromium core. Every tab is its own sandboxed process, and nothing a website does can reach your files.

  1. Sandboxed tabsEach site runs isolated, with no access to your system.
  2. Permissions ask firstCamera, microphone, location and notifications. Hidden third-party frames can’t ask at all.

Good to know

Straight answers before you switch.

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no subscription, nothing to sign into.

What is it built on?

Chromium, the same engine as Chrome, Edge and Brave, so sites work the way you expect.

Why does Windows warn me?

The app isn’t code-signed yet. Choose More info → Run anyway once.

How do updates work?

There’s no auto-update yet. New releases bring Chromium’s latest security fixes, so install them when they appear.

Do Netflix and Spotify work?

Not yet. DRM-protected video and music need a component this build doesn’t include.

Does it support extensions?

Not yet. Shields already cover what most people install an ad blocker for.

Where is my data?

Only on your PC, in %APPDATA%\Atrium Browser. Nothing is synced or sent anywhere.

A site looks broken?

Click the shield and turn Shields down for that site. It reloads instantly.
